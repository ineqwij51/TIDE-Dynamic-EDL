# Limitations and release blockers

- The complete raw-EEG preprocessing and annotation-release pipeline is not available as one public audited workflow. The supported boundary is prepared DE features.
- Raw EEG, private annotations, group identifiers, checkpoints, prediction bundles, and normalization snapshots cannot be distributed here.
- The exact final manuscript framework figure is absent. An approximate figure was not substituted.
- The manuscript author list, ordering, affiliations, ORCIDs, and persistent identifier are unconfirmed, so `CITATION.cff` is intentionally absent.
- No project license has been selected, so `LICENSE` is intentionally absent and the repository must remain private.
- Official baseline bodies and weights have separate upstream terms. Their redistribution and adapter rights require author review.
- The public model/loss/streaming/checkpoint parity gates and a synthetic one-epoch path are tested, but the full five-fold × three-seed training matrix was not rerun during this refactor.
